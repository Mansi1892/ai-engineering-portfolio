01

Constrain probabilistic work

Use models where judgment is valuable; keep calculations, access rules, and validation deterministic.

02

Persist the workflow

Store state, versions, and decisions so long-running agent tasks can recover, resume, and be reviewed.

03

Keep humans in control

Approval gates belong before consequential actions such as outbound communication or plan acceptance.

04

Design failure paths first

Retries, idempotency, fallbacks, and partial-failure reporting are product behavior—not cleanup work.
